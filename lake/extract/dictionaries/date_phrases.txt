# date_phrases.txt - words for the cargo ready date, for lake/extract/Rules.scala
#
# Format: section|phrase|value    (whole words, ignoring case)
#
# month     month names and abbreviations -> month number
# weekday   day names -> ISO day number (Monday = 1)
# relative  words resolved against the message's sent date (UAE time):
#             SENT          the sent date            (ASAP, urgent)
#             SENT+n        n days after it          (today = SENT+0, tomorrow = SENT+1)
#             NEXT_MONDAY   Monday of the following week (next week)
#             IGNORE        not a date, although it contains a date word ("AOG urgent")
#           The longest phrase wins, so "aog urgent" is ignored but "urgent" alone is SENT.
# context   a date right after these words is not the ready date ("as discussed on the
#           phone 29/10" is the date of a call)
#
# Numeric and named dates are recognised by Rules.scala and resolved like this:
#   12/03, 12 March, March 12   next date on or after the sent date with that day and month
#   on the 12th                 that day in the sent month, or next month if already past
#   Thursday                    next Thursday strictly after the sent date
#   12/03/2024, 12.03.2024      that date
# (the same rules as the truth file, generators/messages_gen.py)
